Our Story

Built from a vision to serve — Akani M Projects has grown from a small tools company into a full-service engineering and construction partner for South Africa.

Who We Are

Built to Last

Akani M Projects was founded with a clear intention: to build a contracting company that serves communities across South Africa with quality, reliability, and pride. We started by procuring a small tools and plant operation — because we believed the best way to earn trust was to show up, do the work, and let results speak.

From those early days, we've grown into a multi-disciplinary company delivering civil and electrical engineering, building construction, water drilling, security services, and plant and equipment hire.

We are proudly based in Malamulele, Limpopo — and deeply committed to the communities we operate in. Our motto isn't a slogan. "We Deliver No Matter the Circumstance" is how we operate every single day.
