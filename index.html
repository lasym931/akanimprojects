About Us

Built on Integrity & Grit

Akani M Projects was founded with a singular vision: to become a fully-fledged contracting company that services communities and infrastructure across South Africa. We launched with a plant and small tools division — giving us the operational foundation to grow fast and serve reliably.

Today we deliver civil and electrical engineering, building construction, water drilling, comprehensive security services, and plant & event equipment hire — all from our base in Malamulele, Limpopo.

Answered

What services does Akani M Projects offer?

We offer a comprehensive suite including civil engineering, electrical engineering, building construction, water drilling (borehole), security services, and plant & event equipment hire.

How do I get a project quote?

Fill in the contact form on this page, call us on 072 278 9232, or send us a WhatsApp message. We'll respond within one business day with a detailed, no-obligation quote.

What areas do you serve?

Our primary base is Malamulele, Limpopo. We service clients across the Limpopo province and take on projects across South Africa depending on scope and scale.

Can you handle large-scale construction projects?

Absolutely. Our team, equipment fleet and project management systems are built for both small residential works and major infrastructure contracts. We welcome projects of all scales.

Do you offer equipment hire without an operator?

Yes — plant and equipment can be hired with or without an operator. We have qualified operators available on request to ensure safe and efficient use of all machinery.

Are you registered with the CIDB?

Yes, Akani M Projects is registered and compliant with South African contracting regulations. Please contact us for specific registration details relevant to your tender or project.
